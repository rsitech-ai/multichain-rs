# Support

Multichain is a developer preview. Use
[GitHub issues](https://github.com/rsitech-ai/multichain-rs/issues) for
reproducible, non-sensitive defects and focused feature proposals.

Include:

- the commit or release;
- operating system and architecture;
- the affected connector, processor, or service;
- expected and observed behavior;
- the smallest safe reproduction; and
- the verification commands already run.

Use [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) and
[info@rsitech.ai](mailto:info@rsitech.ai) for vulnerabilities or reports that
contain private infrastructure details. Never post credentials, private keys,
wallet material, internal endpoints, or production chain data in an issue.

RSI Tech does not guarantee support or response times for unreleased branches,
modified builds, undocumented environments, or production deployments.
