# Changelog

All notable public releases of Multichain are documented here.

## 0.1.0 - 2026-07-23

Initial RSI Tech developer preview:

- source-qualified observation, WAL, archive, and replay contracts;
- Bitcoin Core ingestion, UTXO reversal, observer-local mempools, RBF/CPFP,
  backfill, facts, and serving contracts;
- Ethereum Reth/consensus and BSC native-finality domain and replay semantics;
- Solana Yellowstone, fork-qualified identity, selected-account rollback, and
  decoder semantics;
- Redpanda, ClickHouse, PostgreSQL, MinIO, REST, and WebSocket synthetic path;
- deterministic four-chain replay and fault tests;
- bounded local native-client validation for Bitcoin, Ethereum, BSC, Solana,
  and the durable platform; and
- Apache-2.0 public release metadata and checksummed operator bundles.

Production mainnet source deployments, multi-region HA, capacity qualification,
and external provider credentials remain outside this release.
